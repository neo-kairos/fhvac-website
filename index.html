We are a non-profit, volunteer-based ambulance service dedicated to providing high-quality emergency medical care to our local communities in Queens, New York. Our new website is currently under development and will be launching soon.

Volunteer With Us: We are always looking for dedicated communities members who want to make a real difference. Whether you are an experienced EMT or interested in learning and serving, volunteering with FHVAC is a meaningful way to support your neighbors and gain valuable emergency medical experience.
